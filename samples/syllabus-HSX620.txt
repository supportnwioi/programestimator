Course Title: HSX 620 Sexual Health Across the Lifespan
Fall 2024 · 3 semester credits · Live online (Zoom), synchronous

Weekly schedule
Week 1: Developmental sexuality across the life course (6 hours)

Week 3: Medical factors, illness and disability and sexuality (6 hours)
Week 4: Substance use and sexuality
Week 5: Sexuality and technology, social media and generative AI (4 hours)
Week 6: Sexual exploitation, abuse and assault (6 hours)
