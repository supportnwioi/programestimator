## Site-wide

- **Tier names.**
  - The AASECT Certification page says "Tier III Modular Track". The Compare page says "Tier III: Flexible" and "Flexible Certification Options".
  - Pick one name; the track finder uses **Modular**. Use **Tier I · Comprehensive**, **Tier II · Core**, **Tier III · Modular** everywhere.
- **Calculator link.** Add a button near each tier summary and in the main navigation, under AASECT Certification: **Find my track & see tuition**, linking to the track finder.
- **Booking link.** "Schedule Free Admissions Call" currently points to three different places:
  - `https://student.nwioi.com/inquire` (Compare page)
  - `https://student.nwioi.com/` (AASECT Certification page)
  - `https://www.nwioi.com/nwioi-application` (AASECT Certification page)

  Choose one booking destination and set it as the admissions scheduling URL under Admin → Links & internal rates.

## Sex Education Program page

This page currently reads as clinical sexology training for healthcare providers.
- It says "we've intentionally chosen not to offer a comprehensive program", which contradicts Tier I · Comprehensive.
- It describes a different structure: "TOP5" pricing, $8,441.25 for Sex Therapist and $7,263.75 for Sex Counselor, with Fall/Spring Advanced classes.

Either retire or rename it (for example, "Clinical Sexology Education (TOP5)") and point it to the tiers. Then give the new Sexuality Educator pathway its own page or section:

> **Sexuality Educator Certification Track**
> For teachers, health educators, trainers, and community educators working toward AASECT certification as a Sexuality Educator. Complete 150 hours of education — core sexuality knowledge plus sexuality education skills training — and **design and deliver a sexuality education program with feedback from an AASECT Certified Sexuality Educator Supervisor**. Supervision includes five individual hours with your primary supervisor and 20 group hours in ten two-hour sessions, over at least nine months. SAR included in Tier I.
> **[Find my track & see tuition]**  **[Book a free admissions call]**

Remove "Sex Therapist / Sex Counselor TOP5" pricing from any Educator-facing copy, and replace "Sex Counselor" with the credential name "Sexuality Counselor".
